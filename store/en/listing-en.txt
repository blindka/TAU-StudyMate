Your study planner: tasks, Moodle sync, weekly readings, reminders and focus

=== Full description (max 4000) ===
StudyMate keeps all of your university studies in one place – no missed deadlines, no folders full of PDFs.

📚 Courses and materials
• All your courses by year and semester, with lecturers and credits
• Summaries, recordings and links for every course
• "What am I missing" list for notes or recordings you still need
• Grades and a credit-weighted average

✅ Tasks and deadlines
• Pick the exact due date and time, edit anytime
• Weekly recurring tasks (homework every week)
• Month calendar view to spot busy weeks
• Search, filters, and export to WhatsApp or email

🔄 Moodle sync
• Paste your Moodle calendar link once
• Deadlines arrive automatically every day – no duplicates, extensions update in place

📄 Weekly readings
• Upload the week's articles and slides as PDFs, per course
• Read them inside the app, check them off, bookmark pages and keep notes
• On-device summaries (supported phones) and English → Hebrew translation

🔔 Reminders
• Choose when to be reminded: 3 days, 1 day, 3 hours or 1 hour before
• Reminders survive a phone restart

⏱️ Focus timer and habits
• Pomodoro 25/45/60 or a free stopwatch, keeps running with the screen locked
• Study streak and insights from your own data

🏠 Home-screen widget with your next deadlines
🌐 English and Hebrew

🔒 Privacy
Everything stays on your phone. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.

Note: StudyMate is an independent app built by a student. It is not an official Tel Aviv University app and is not affiliated with it.
